Backyard

Move WASD or the left thumb. Look drag on empty space (or press L for mouse-look).

Grab press and drag a thing; let go to fling it. Tap a thing to carry it, F or Throw to throw it, G to drop it. Wheel or pinch pushes it away or pulls it closer.

Carry the box camera from the picnic table. E or Raise looks through it, C or Snap takes a photo.

O orbit view. V sprinkler. T skip an hour. P pause.
