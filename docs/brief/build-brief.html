How fast tokens burn

You asked how fast it's possible to burn. The fastest stretch was the design panel plus the spikes: about 14 Opus agents at once took 37% of a 5-hour window and 9% of the weekly limit per hour. At that pace a 5-hour window is gone in under three hours. The meter covers the whole account, so other sessions running that night are in it too.

Predictions ran hot

The design phase was priced at 30% of a window and used 56%, a 1.9× over-run, because the spikes overlapped it. The budget ledger now raises the Opus multiplier for fan-outs.

The three-hour flat line

From about 4 pm to 7 pm almost nothing ran. The cloud sessions I requested never started, since they needed a click in the app. I withdrew them and ran the build locally instead.

Cheap passes at the end

After your note, the last two review passes used Sonnet at 2 agents a time. They fixed onboarding and phone layouts in all four unfinished games for about 7% of a window.

What's still rough

  • Nothing has been tested on a real phone or GPU. Every check ran in headless Chromium with software WebGL.
  • Nobody has listened to the 200 synthesized sounds.
  • Plates hasn't had a clue-legibility pass at each loupe power, and that check decides whether the mystery is fair.
  • The Studio makes about 420 draw calls against a 250 budget, so phones may stutter.
  • Most "should have" features were cut. Each game's leftovers are listed in docs/build/ in the repo.
